Privacy & terms

Last updated 20 November 2026

Wayfarer plans trips in your browser. There are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and Wayfarer itself sets no cookies.

What stays on your device

  • Saved trips are kept in your browser's local storage, so they open without a connection. Delete them from Saved, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • Places from OpenStreetMap are cached in your browser for up to seven days, so planning the same city again is quicker.
  • Places from Google are never stored. A trip planned with Google data is saved as its search only — destination, dates, people and transport — and is planned again when you open it.

What is sent, and to whom

To build a plan, Wayfarer sends only what each service needs:

  • Open-Meteo receives the destination you type, to find it, and its position and your dates, for the forecast.
  • Google Places receives the destination's position and how you'll get around, through Wayfarer's own server, to find sights, restaurants and places to stay.
  • OpenStreetMap servers (overpass-api.de, overpass.kumi.systems, overpass.private.coffee) and Wikipedia receive the destination's position, as alternative sources of places. Wikipedia also provides the city summary.
  • OpenFreeMap and Google Maps serve the map you see. When a Google map is shown, it loads from Google, which may set its own cookies.
  • Booking.com receives your destination, dates and party size only if you click Check prices.
  • Netlify hosts Wayfarer and, like any web host, keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses.

Each service handles what it receives under its own privacy policy. Your flight times never leave your browser.

A shared trip link contains the destination, dates, number of people, transport and the place's approximate position — anyone you send it to can see those.

Terms of use

  • Wayfarer is provided free and as is, without warranty. Plans are suggestions built from public and third-party data.
  • Travel times are estimates, not routes. Opening hours, prices, ratings and availability can be wrong or out of date — check with the place or the booking site before you rely on them.
  • Wayfarer does not sell anything or take bookings. Anything you book on another site is between you and that site.
